2)Your project has three different tasks: speech recognition, language translation, and structured data extraction.

step-1 speech to text
Our application first records the social worker's voice using Expo Audio in React Native. The audio is uploaded
to our Python backend, where Pydub preprocesses it by converting it to mono, reducing long silent sections, normalizing
volume, and resampling it to 16 kHz. We then use the Python SpeechRecognition library with Google's speech-recognition
service and specify `hi-IN` for Hindi. The service returns the recognized Hindi text, which is used by the next stage of
our report-generation pipeline.


3)Our project uses a three-stage AI pipeline. First, the mobile application records the social worker's
 voice using Expo Audio and uploads it to our Flask backend. The backend preprocesses the audio using Pydub and
  converts speech into Hindi text using the Python SpeechRecognition library with Google's speech-recognition service
   configured for Hindi, or hi-IN.

Second, the recognized Hindi text is passed to Gemini. Our prompt instructs Gemini to translate the complete statement
 into English and extract the relevant social-work information. We do not need a separate translation API because translation
  and information extraction are handled in the same Gemini request.

Third, Gemini returns structured JSON containing fields such as worker name, activity, number of people visited,
 health concerns, assistance provided, referrals, and follow-up information. Python parses the JSON, checks the required 
 fields, and sends the report back to the mobile app. The worker can review the generated report before saving the reviewed 
 activity to the database.

 5)About social workers
 A social worker is a person who helps individuals, families, and communities solve social problems and 
 access the support and services they need.
Their main goal is to improve people's well-being, protect vulnerable people, and connect them with government
 schemes, healthcare, education, and other support services.

 Social workers may work for:
- NGOs and charitable organizations
- Government social welfare departments
- Hospitals and community health programmes
- Schools and child protection organizations
- Rural development and women’s empowerment programmes
- Organizations supporting older adults and people with disabilities


6)tech stack
Our project uses **React Native, TypeScript, and Expo** for the mobile frontend, and **Python with Flask**
 for the backend. We use **Expo Audio** to record voice, **Pydub** for audio preprocessing, and the Python 
 **SpeechRecognition library with Google's speech-recognition service** to convert Hindi speech into text. 
 We use **Google Gemini through the Google Gen AI SDK** to translate the Hindi transcription into English and
 extract structured social-work information. The backend provides REST APIs for communication, while the system supports 
  saving reviewed activity records and generating PDF and Excel reports.
