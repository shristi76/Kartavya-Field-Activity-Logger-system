- users stores each account. id is its primary key, and worker_id is unique so workers can sign in with their own ID. Passwords are stored as password_hash, not plain text. role distinguishes workers from admins.
- field_logs stores activity reports. id is the primary key, and user_id links each report to its owner in users. This lets the app show workers their own records and lets admins manage records according to their role.
Other fields store the report details, such as created_at, hindi_text, translated_text, activity, beneficiaries, and location. audio_file and photo_file store file names or paths, keeping the media itself outside the database. The photo field can hold multiple filenames for one report.
